How this works · registry · data

What the check looks for: can crawlers reach your pages (robots.txt, sitemap.xml), can they read who you are (structured data, description, canonical), and what your site says to each platform's crawlers — Google, Bing (feeds ChatGPT & Copilot), Yandex, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Common Crawl. Every problem comes with a one-click fix: the exact snippet, ready to paste. No ranking promises — search engines decide that.

Reaching beyond Google and Bing: one IndexNow ping covers Bing + Yandex; AI answers read you through their crawlers — the check reads your stance toward each. Stance is measurable now; citation is measured by our re-check loop, never promised.

The impact registry — every submission logged with URL, verdict, deciding keywords and timestamp. 🌿 usable (accepted, audit ran) · 🍂 not usable (declined, refusal logged as data) · 🌱 queued for human review.

registry: Supabase (PostgreSQL), provisioned and hosted via QwenWork Pages · keyword-gate v1, human review pending · first pass automated; a human reads every accepted audit · nothing here promises ranking
