## Decision taken (D9, 19 Nov — founder's ruling)

   a real named practitioner cannot be in our materials: hallucination risk

   and materials, and "explain any part of your submission if asked" makes
   "did they approve this?" an easy question to lose.
2. **Measured layer = real** (Tier A + B): dated engine answers, gaps, our
   analysis, public observation only. Carries the Feasibility-Viability 25% +
   Innovation credibility.
3. **Fixed layer = fictitious**, profiled from Tier A, labelled synthetic in
   the video and in the site copy ("built from patterns observed across SG
   craft trades", never "we did this for X"). Carries the working product.
4. A real Tier A engagement, if it happens, is the post-11-Dec story, not the
   hack build.
