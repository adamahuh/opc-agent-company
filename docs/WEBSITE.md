## 1. Positioning line (hero)

**Hero line (v0 — final copy after the D12 positioning pass):**
> **Be the answer. Not just the article.**
> For the trades, restoration projects and community organisations whose work
> matters — and that AI search keeps missing, or misnaming.

Support line:
> We measure how visible you are across AI engines (ChatGPT, Gemini,
> Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews), search and social discovery —
> then build what's missing so you become the source the answer cites.

Why not the obvious line: "AI-powered SEO" is what every competitor says;
"become the answer they cite" is Hashmeta's own category stated in the
buyer's words, and the 15% is judged by looking at the page.

## 3. Copy block (paste-ready for Hashmeta AI)

Input, not page. The page still has to come out of their tool for the 15% to
count, so this block waits on the account question above.

| # | Field | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero | *Be the answer. Not just the article.* |
| 2 | Sub-hero | *For the trades, restoration projects and community organisations whose work matters — and that AI search keeps missing, or misnaming.* |
| 3 | Problem (~90 words) | Ask where to learn a craft, buy a traditionally made piece, or support a restoration project, and the answer names a journalist's article, a blog, a marketplace. It usually doesn't name the person doing the work. That invisibility costs bookings, buyers, volunteers — and successors. |
| 4 | How it works, 3 steps | **Measure** — 10+ surfaces, dated answers, cited share of answer, graded invisible / mediated / thin / owned. **Build** — ≤10 high-confidence assets: the definitional page, the expert-led page, structured data where true, a named author with credentials, in the languages your community actually searches in. **Prove** — re-measured on a schedule, reported in plain language, and you approve anything outbound before it goes out. |
| 5 | Proof | Graded before-states on real cases (with permission after 23 Nov), the synthetic subject labelled as such, the before/after diff with our three honesty columns. |
| 6 | Who it's for | Cultural councils, museums, craft guilds and federations, heritage and sustainability programmes, tourism bodies — and the custodians inside their portfolios. |
| 7 | Pricing / CTA | One **Rescue** per trade or acquisition, fixed scope; or a yearly **programme** across the portfolio. CTA: *"Send us one thing you keep alive. We'll show you how visible it is."* |
| 8 | What we refuse | No bulk AI page generation. No words published under a practitioner's name without their sign-off. No generated health/legal/financial claims for care services. These are product rules, not values copy. |
| 9 | Footer | Contact, organisation, privacy, "built with Hashmeta AI". |

Tone rule borrowed from Hashmeta's own site: concrete numbers, one-sentence
claims, no hype, no logo wall. Engine names go in prose, not badges — a badge
wall reads as a tool claim where naming surfaces and citing primary sources
reads as method.
