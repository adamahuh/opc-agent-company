## Where the product currently stands

- **ICP direction:** buyer = institutions with a preservation mandate
  (museums, cultural councils, guilds, heritage/trade programmes, tourism
  bodies); beneficiary = trades, remaining practitioners, local ecosystem.
- **Actor model (PRD §9b):** asset-holder contact is a **hard requirement**
  (consent + verification + byline) and is also the mechanism that makes
  sources citable — Hashmeta's own 9-factor list, #9: bylined authors +
  credentials + Author schema. Buyer contact is the sale. Beneficiary and
  audience are *not* relationships we manage.
- **Requirements draft:** FR1 run real engine queries + dated evidence,
  FR2 authorship of missing assets, FR3 scheduled re-measure + diff report,
  FR4 approval gate/audit trail, FR5 plain-language scorecard, FR6 Hashmeta
  website. FR1/2/3 = MUST.
- **Innovation risk register:** must answer "you're selling Hashmeta's own
  service." Working answer: we serve the segment no agency can bill
  economically, with agent delivery collapsing unit cost.
