reconciliation requests to role chats — explicit invocation is what authorises those messages),

or publication authority), **`ANA`** (one combined LD review per section, reporting *meets

**`SS`**, **`Vision`** (real rendered screenshots; static mockups cannot establish motion),
**`visualise`** / **`vis3`**, **`placeholder`**, **`KIV`**.

**Authority change not to miss:** the old "never commit/push/deploy unless specifically asked"
line is **gone**, replaced by *"apply existing task authority to commits, pushes, packaging and
publication… do not invent repeated permission gates."* Destructive actions and production
config keep their own limits. `Automata` now states: **a source push alone does not establish a
website deployment.**

**New agent-message protocol** — required on every agent-authored relay (adopted for the
marketing chat from now on):


Record **sent / acknowledged / completed** separately. A forwarded request retains its original
authority and limits.
