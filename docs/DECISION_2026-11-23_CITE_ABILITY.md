# D24 — Cite-ability positioning unlock (23 Nov 2026)

**Status:** LOCKED (founder-approved, 23 Nov 2026).

## Decision

embold'r's positioning shifts from "SEO & AI visibility for impact businesses" to the **impact-verification layer**: *"We make impact itself cite-able — machine-readable, traceable to its source, verifiable by buyers."*

## Rationale

- What must be cite-able is the **impact claim**, not just the business entity. Impact consumers (and B2B/B2G buyers) decide on provenance, custody, verification — not rankings.
- Traditional SEO measures traffic and page rank; it cannot make provenance, craft or stewardship machine-readable.
- Three-buyer ladder: **practitioner** (free check → Rescue) · **CSR-driven company** ("Verified impact portfolio": fund rescues, receive verified reporting) · **institution** (annual account).
- CSR/ESG companies are **buyers, never registry subjects** — resolves the D-SEO gate tension (impact = heritage + environment only).

## Guardrails (unchanged + new)

- No competitor names in public copy — differentiation is framed as "what impact work needs that traditional SEO can't give." Competitor comparison is a founder answer for judges, not site copy.
- No overclaiming verification infrastructure: "machine-readable and traceable to source — verification is the buyer's job."
- All prior locks stay: no scores, gate = heritage+environment, refusals-as-data, pricing on request, ≤10 assets, sign-off.

## Implementation (23 Nov, live)

- Home: eyebrow/sub repositioned; "Verified impact portfolio" tier added; meta updated.
- Tool page: differentiation paragraph (no names); "Use it" beat = "what machines can read, know, and cite about your impact."
- Engine (nxtb2cwd V8): platform-specific "Where:" instructions per fix (Squarespace/WordPress/Wix/Shopify detected); impact-schema `knowsAbout` paste-in with own-words placeholders; internal notes removed from customer letters; cite-ability close.
